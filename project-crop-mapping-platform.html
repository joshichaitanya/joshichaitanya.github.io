Case study

Large-Scale Geospatial Data Platform

A map-heavy, interactive platform helping users track tens of millions of records at scale — surfacing weather and pricing data alongside the map — shipped ahead of a fixed seasonal deadline.

ReactJS TypeScript NodeJS PostgreSQL AWS

What I did

  • Led a 5-person frontend team as most senior engineer to deliver a map-heavy, data-intensive platform ahead of a fixed seasonal business deadline.
  • Negotiated MVP scope with design/business stakeholders to protect the timeline, and unblocked mid-level engineers via pair programming and structured refinement ceremonies.
  • Co-owned the highest-risk Leaflet.js module and engineered viewport-based map/list synchronization to eliminate browser freezing at scale for a data-heavy mapping interface.
  • Shipped on time to 10K+ daily active users managing tens of millions of records total, sustained through frontend caching, memoization, and virtualization.
  • Raised data reliability by ~25% by automating third-party data sync via scheduled jobs.

Architecture

A viewport-driven map/list sync layer sits between Leaflet.js and a virtualized plot list, so only in-view plots hydrate at a time — keeping tens of millions of records interactive without freezing the browser. A NodeJS BFF aggregates map, environmental and pricing data for the frontend.
