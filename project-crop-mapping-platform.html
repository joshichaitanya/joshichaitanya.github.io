Case study

Large-Scale Crop Mapping Platform

A map-heavy platform helping growers track tens of millions of plot records at scale, shipped ahead of a fixed pre-spring harvest deadline.

React TypeScript Node.js AI AWS

What I did

  • Led a 5-person frontend team as most senior engineer to deliver a map-heavy crop management platform ahead of a fixed pre-spring harvest deadline.
  • Negotiated MVP scope with design/business stakeholders to protect the timeline, and unblocked mid-level engineers via pair programming and structured refinement ceremonies.
  • Co-owned the highest-risk Leaflet.js module and engineered viewport-based map/list synchronization to eliminate browser freezing at scale for a data-heavy mapping interface.
  • Shipped on time to 30K+ growers managing up to 1,000 plots each (tens of millions of plot records), sustained through frontend caching, memoization, and virtualization.
  • Raised data reliability by 25% by automating commodity pricing sync via CRON jobs from third-party APIs.

Architecture

A viewport-driven map/list sync layer sits between Leaflet.js and a virtualized plot list, so only in-view plots hydrate at a time — keeping tens of millions of records interactive without freezing the browser. A Node.js BFF aggregates plot, weather and commodity pricing data for the frontend.
